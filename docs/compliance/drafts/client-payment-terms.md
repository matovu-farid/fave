## Settled facts and product decisions

The legal supplier and intended merchant is **FIDEXA ENTERPRISES - SMC LIMITED**, registration **80034103105807**. Fave undertakes to provide the trip through the agreed car and driver; it is not described as a driver-only contract. Give the selected provider this principal role when obtaining its written funds-flow approval. Corporate particulars and the owner's business contact are in the finished client terms; support is **farid@fidexa.org**, **+256 705 222 144**.

Show the car-specific saved estimate first; staff call the assigned driver for those dates after client intent, without a deposit or pending-request hold. Confirmation preserves the exact estimate price and holds the car and driver for 24 hours. Checkout atomically replaces that hold with a 30-minute hold from checkout start. Acceptance and deposit verification precede the communicated booking confirmation received by the client, subject to mandatory formation rules. An expired hold requires a new availability check. This formation wording remains a drafting resolution for exact-version review.

A driver enters one Kampala–Jinja round-trip benchmark price per car. Derive that car's transport rate from the approved reference road-distance basis, price each actual direction independently, and show outbound and return separately. There is no universal UGX 2,000/km fallback. Driver lodging is separate, uses the driver's registered nightly rate across destinations and the saved Kampala-date night count, and never includes client lodging. Monday to Tuesday is one night.

Client prices are UGX rounded to whole shillings, with positive half-up ties per transport line and deposit, totals summing rounded lines and the balance derived by subtraction. Preserve the precise benchmark ratio. **Payment-processing fees are included in the quoted total; no extra processing fee is added at checkout.** Actual merchant fees, taxes and fee-bearing configuration remain subject to provider confirmation. No fee amount is invented here.

The configurable 50% deposit remains a proposed default, not an effective owner-approved percentage. Exact deposit, balance and due events must be disclosed and accepted in the quote before commitment. The intended balance is after trip completion; completion evidence and dispute rules remain unresolved. Do not collect an unaccepted amount or automatically charge disputed completion. An accepted extension uses the saved driver-lodging rate; payment for added nights is due immediately on acceptance after availability is confirmed.

Fave pays each driver's itemized full pay for each trip day that morning from business funds, including that day's lodging component. It is not a separate lodging advance or trip-long lump sum. A daily payment already sent is kept if the trip is cancelled; stop future daily payments after the trip ends. Verify trip and daily-payment records at completion, with no routine later driver balance. This internal arrangement never reduces a client's lawful refund.

The owner selected the commercial deposit schedule: full refund at least 72 hours before pickup, half at least 24 but less than 72 hours, and no commercial deposit refund inside 24 hours. Mandatory cancellation rights and reasonable-compensation limits take priority. If Fave cannot provide the accepted car and driver, return the full deposit and other payments for the unavailable service. Follow the finished cancellation policy for notice, receipt timing, statutory deadlines, partial service and all-payment remedies. Provider posting or recovery delays do not extend Fave's legal deadline.
