# Fave Client Payment Terms

**Status:** Internal client-payment terms, prepared 12 October 2026 and updated 13 October 2026. The owner reports that counsel has approved the payment-provider terms. The owner chose a marketplace-style product flow: the client pays Fave; Fave breaks the driver’s pay down by trip day and sends that day’s itemized full pay in the morning from Fave’s business funds. The daily total includes that day’s accommodation fee and is the driver’s complete pay for that day, not an advance toward later pay. Fave does not send a separate nightly lodging payment or a trip-long lump sum. If the trip is cancelled after a daily payment is sent, the driver keeps it; no later daily payments are sent after the trip ends. After completion, Fave verifies payment and trip records; no routine additional driver balance is due. “Accommodation” means the driver’s lodging of any kind, not hotels only; Fave does not arrange or pay for client/passenger lodging. This document records product direction but is not an executed provider offer and is not effective for customer transactions until the legal merchant, provider configuration, launch items below, and exact version shown before payment are confirmed.

**Counsel review:** The owner confirms counsel approved the payment-provider terms on 12 October 2026. The written advice has not been attached to this repository. That approval is recorded separately from provider selection, signature and onboarding, the final merchant/funds-flow configuration, provider-specific fees and refund capabilities, and any required regulatory approvals.

## 1. Who takes payment

Fave’s product model is that the client pays Fave for the booking; the driver’s full pay is broken down by trip day and paid in the morning from Fave’s business funds. The itemized daily amount includes that day’s accommodation fee, calculated using the saved nightly rate and trip-night count; it is the complete pay for that day, not an advance toward later pay or a separate nightly lodging transfer. If the trip is cancelled after a daily payment is sent, the driver keeps it; no later daily payments are sent after the trip ends. After completion, Fave verifies payment and trip records; no routine additional driver balance is due. The exact daily breakdown, rate amount, evidence, and available operating balance remain open and must be finalized before live bookings. “Accommodation” means the driver’s lodging of any kind, not hotels only; Fave does not arrange or pay for client/passenger lodging. The legal merchant, transport-service provider, and Fave’s legal role must be confirmed and shown before the client confirms an order. This product choice does not mean funds are held in escrow or determine Fave’s regulatory status. Pesapal Uganda Limited has confirmed by email that its Uganda service supports online MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money, and bank-card collection in UGX, subject to Fave’s onboarding, KYC, and compliance review. Pesapal has asked Fave to clarify whether Fave acts as principal or marketplace/intermediary before it confirms the appropriate settlement and KYC structure.

Until Fave has selected a provider and confirmed the actual merchant and funds flow in writing, this draft does not promise that Pesapal (or another named provider) will process a particular booking. Fave will offer only payment methods enabled for the actual merchant account and displayed at checkout. Fave will not accept live customer funds until the provider and counsel have confirmed that the selected merchant configuration supports the owner-directed collection, full daily driver payments with accommodation included, and trip-end record reconciliation with no routine later driver balance.

## 2. Price and payment schedule

Before the client places an order or starts payment, Fave will show the accepted trip details, the total price in UGX, the amount due now, any remaining balance and when it is due, and any applicable taxes or fees. The total shown at confirmation is the amount the client authorizes. Fave will not add an undisclosed payment surcharge at checkout.

The current product direction is to request a configurable deposit, with a default of 50% of the accepted quote. The remaining balance is due after the trip is complete. The precise deposit amount and due time will be displayed on the accepted quote; this draft does not itself make the 50% default effective. The final balance will be shown to the client before it is charged. A disputed completion or amount pauses collection until Fave records an operations decision.

The client may pay using MTN or Airtel mobile money or a local bank card when the selected method is available for that payment. The deposit and balance are separate transactions. Fave will record the provider reference, amount, currency, payment stage, status, and timestamp against the booking and provide a receipt or in-app payment record.

## 3. Trip changes and added nights

For a date-based trip, Fave counts nights using Kampala calendar dates from the trip start/check-in date up to, but not including, the return/check-out date. Monday to Tuesday is one night. The quote shows the dates, night count, applicable per-night rate, and amount.

Fave will not silently increase an accepted total. If the client asks to extend the trip and add driver lodging nights, Fave will calculate the added amount using the driver-lodging per-night rate saved in the accepted quote, show the number of added nights and the revised total, and ask the client to accept before amending the booking. When the client accepts, payment for the added nights is due immediately using a method then available. The booking record will retain the original accepted quote and calculation inputs, the revised dates and night-count calculation, the revised quote, the client's acceptance, and the resulting payment reference. Fave does not provide or arrange lodging for the client/passengers.

Other changes that affect price or service require a revised quote and the client's acceptance before the change is charged or applied. A provider, driver, or Fave cannot add an amount that was not shown and accepted through this process.

## 4. Payment processing and confirmation

Payment is not complete merely because a client has opened a provider page, approved a mobile-money prompt, or returned to Fave. Fave will treat payment as received only after it verifies the transaction with the provider and records a successful status. If a payment is pending, Fave will show that status and will not ask the client to pay again until the result has been checked. If a payment fails or expires, Fave will explain the next step and show any new amount or quote before another attempt.

Payments are processed by the provider and the client's bank, card issuer, or mobile-money operator. Their security checks, service availability, and processing times may affect the transaction. Fave will not ask a client to send a card PIN, mobile-money PIN, password, or one-time authentication code to Fave by chat, email, phone, or support form. The client should enter authentication details only into the payment method's own secure prompt.

## 5. Refunds, cancellations, and reversals

The amount a client may receive back depends on the approved cancellation, trip-change, and service-failure policy shown before payment and any rights the client has under applicable law. **Owner-confirmed cancellation schedule recorded 13 October 2026:** refund the full deposit for cancellation at least 72 hours before pickup; refund half of the deposit for cancellation at least 24 but less than 72 hours before pickup; and do not refund the deposit for cancellation less than 24 hours before pickup. This schedule still requires Ugandan counsel's review and approval before publication or collection. No-show consequences, including any grace period and required contact attempts, have not been decided. The remedy if Fave cancels or cannot assign an eligible driver and vehicle also remains to be finalized. Partial-performance treatment, refund timing, and treatment of provider fees remain open. Fave will not describe a payment as non-refundable or deduct a provider fee from a refund unless the applicable rule has been disclosed and approved.

When a refund is approved, Fave will initiate it through the original payment route where supported and will show the refund amount, initiation date, and status. The provider or financial institution controls the final posting time. If the original method cannot accept the refund, Fave will contact the client to confirm a lawful alternative before paying by another route. A pending refund is not represented as completed until the provider confirms it.

If a client does not recognize a charge or believes a payment was taken incorrectly, contact Fave using the support details shown in the app and include the booking or receipt reference. This does not limit the client's right to contact their bank, card issuer, mobile-money operator, regulator, or other appropriate body.

## 6. Provider-specific information and availability

Fave currently has a Pesapal Uganda merchant sign-up pack and merchant-contract template, but has not signed it or received a completed merchant-specific fee schedule. Pesapal's 6 October 2026 response says the required collection methods are supported, subject to onboarding, and states that its commercial and compliance teams must first review Fave's merchant-versus-marketplace model. It describes settlement, driver payouts, refunds, and chargebacks as matters to confirm after that review. The accompanying rate details are incomplete or inconsistent with the unfilled schedule, so no rate, tax treatment, settlement time, reserve, or refund fee from those materials is promised to the client here.

If Pesapal is selected and activated, Fave will update this policy with the provider's confirmed role, enabled payment methods, disclosed fees, refund route, and other customer-relevant processing information before accepting live payments. The provider's separate merchant contract governs Fave's relationship with that provider; it does not replace the client-facing booking terms or reduce any client rights.

## 7. Records and support

Fave will keep an auditable record of each accepted quote and revision, the calculation inputs, the policy version, the payment stage and provider reference, provider status changes, receipts, and any refund or dispute decision. Payment credentials and authentication secrets will not be stored in booking records.

For payment questions or errors, use **[in-app support route / support email / telephone]** and provide the payment receipt or booking reference. Fave will acknowledge the request and explain the next step. The final published terms must identify the responsible support contact and its operating hours.

- Define the nightly lodging rate used in the client quote and the itemized full daily driver-pay breakdown, payout route, and required evidence. The owner directs Fave to send the driver’s complete pay for that day in the morning, including that day’s accommodation fee. If a trip is cancelled after a daily payment is sent, the driver keeps it; no later daily payments are sent after the trip ends. Counsel and the provider must confirm this daily payout and record-reconciliation flow, operating-balance controls, client refunds, and disclosures are permitted for the actual arrangement. - Name the support contact, publish the effective date and version, and ensure the app displays the complete price, merchant/provider identity, payment timing, cancellation/refund policy, and current terms before the client orders or pays.
- Complete provider KYC, production method activation, sandbox and reconciliation testing, and any regulatory/legal approval required for the chosen arrangement. Do not collect live customer payments until these checks are complete.
