### 2. What Fave offers

Fave allows a signed-in client to request a multi-day private-car trip with pickup in Kampala, Wakiso or Mukono, an in-country destination, selected trip dates and party/luggage needs, and a choice among eligible vehicle listings. A map/address eligibility check and route estimate support planning; a route estimate is not a promise about actual road conditions, exact duration, or final driver route.

The client selects a vehicle listing, not a driver. A specific driver is assigned only after **[approved assignment event and responsible party]**. Driver identity/contact details will be released only at **[approved event, consistent with issue #12 and privacy/security review]**. Clients must not use the listing to contact, identify or select a driver outside the approved workflow.

### 3. Request, quote, review and order

Before an order is placed, Fave will display the complete transaction for review and correction, including:

- pickup address/pin and destination;
- start/end dates and time window **[define]**;
- party size and luggage needs;
- selected car facts and approved photos;
- route distance/time estimate and its limitations;
- total price, currency, taxes, every Fave/driver/provider fee, deposit and remaining balance;
- who supplies the trip, trip-service description, cancellation/refund process and service timing;
- the current terms, privacy, booking, payment, cancellation and safety documents; and
- the contact-sharing choice and the point when contact details are disclosed.

You may correct the request or withdraw before placing the order. The app will preserve the quote's amount, currency, fare-policy version, expiry, vehicle, dates, route inputs, accepted document versions/languages and booking events. If the quote expires or materially changes, Fave will show the difference and request renewed confirmation before taking the next payment/order action.

### 4. When a booking is formed — unresolved

An availability hold and a booking contract may be different events. Counsel and Fidexa LLC must define when an order is submitted, when the selected vehicle is held, when the booking is accepted by the supplier, when a deposit becomes payable, when the trip contract is binding, and what happens if the supplier does not accept. Proposed event sequence: **[complete with approved quote/deposit/driver-assignment flow]**. No production deposit may be accepted while this clause is incomplete.

Fave will recheck vehicle availability and quote validity at confirmation. A hold expires **[period and exact expiry event]** if payment or supplier confirmation is incomplete. Expiration, payment failure, supplier unavailability and changed price handling are governed by the approved policy version shown before order.

### 5. Prices, payment and receipts — unresolved

Fare calculation, fare components, taxes, fees, currency, deposit amount, payment method, due dates, balance collection, refund route, failed payments and receipts are **[not determined]**. The final disclosure must state the full price and payment method. Payments will be processed using **[licensed PSP / merchant of record and flow; counsel to classify under the National Payment Systems Act]**. Fave will not ask for a card PIN, mobile-money PIN, password or one-time authentication code in chat or a support call.

Any money held pending assignment or trip performance must be attributed to the correct merchant/custodian, protected and reconciled under the approved flow. No escrow, trust, insurance or guaranteed-refund claim is made by this draft.

### 6. Client cancellation, changes and refund — counsel must complete

Cancellation windows, cancellation charges, client no-show, provider cancellation, itinerary changes, vehicle substitution, partial performance, disruption, refund timing, payment-provider charges and dispute process are **[not determined]**. The final policy must state these rules clearly before the order and preserve rights provided by law. Do not use a general waiver or label a payment “non-refundable” without counsel's written approval.

Electronic Transactions Act section 23 includes online disclosures and a 14-day cancellation remedy in specified circumstances; subsection 23(7)(j) excludes specified-date transport from that section 23(3) remedy. Section 24 has a separate cancellation provision, and section 27 invalidates terms that exclude rights in Part IV. How these provisions apply to a particular booking, deposit, supplier structure or consumer remains for counsel. This summary does not state that any statutory right applies or does not apply to a particular Fave transaction.

### 7. Client account and contact sharing

Provide an accurate name and phone number for trip contact. Phone ownership verification is not required in V1. Assignment-time sharing of the client's phone and trip details is a separate, specific choice, shown with recipient, purpose and timing. Declining that optional contact sharing **[effect on booking to be defined; do not silently bundle consent]**. Emergency disclosures and legally required disclosures will be handled under the privacy notice and law.

Do not use another person's account, submit another person's phone number without authority, harass a driver, or share driver/client contact information outside the trip purpose. For account access, correction or complaints contact **[support contact]**.

### 8. Vehicle, driver and safety information

Only approved vehicles and client-facing photos are displayed. Vehicle facts and capacity are based on provider-submitted and reviewed information; the final policy will say what has and has not been independently verified. Fave will not imply that a photo or platform approval is a government inspection or a safety guarantee.

Follow applicable road-safety requirements and reasonable instructions. For immediate danger, contact the relevant emergency service and then **[24-hour Fave contact]**. Report collision, injury, harassment, crime, breakdown, missed pickup, vehicle mismatch, lost property or other safety concern through **[in-app/telephone route]**. Do not publish another person's identity, ID, live itinerary or private contact details.

No term in this draft waives statutory rights, eliminates a party's legal duties, or substitutes for operational safeguarding. Counsel must approve any allocation of safety, insurance, incident and assistance responsibilities.

The client onboarding flow requests a legal name and phone number for account and trip administration. Phone ownership verification is not required in V1. Fave does not request clients to upload a National ID, passport, or refugee ID for these workflows. Any later client identity check would require a separate necessity, lawful-basis, notice, and counsel review before collection.

### 12. Recipients and safeguards

Information may be disclosed to the assigned transport provider only when needed for the accepted trip and at the disclosed time; to the payment provider for collection/refund; to support and incident responders; to hosting/authentication/maps vendors under documented instructions; to insurers/advisers where needed for a claim; or to an authority where lawful. Before launch, name vendor categories, locations, retention and transfers, and provide the required notices.

Proposed safeguards: minimize trip data, restrict access by role, encrypt in transit and at rest, keep private coordinates and contact information out of public vehicle listings, audit access, avoid sensitive log content, define vendor deletion/backup controls, and test access/correction/deletion and breach-response paths.

### 13. Retention and rights

The final schedule is not approved. Regulation 22(2) of the Digital Networks Regulations says licensees retain customer data at least five years from collection. Counsel must determine the categories and starting events covered and reconcile that requirement with Data Protection and Privacy Act purpose-limited retention and secure deletion at expiry. No shorter period or indefinite retention is approved in this draft.

Subject to law and identity verification, you may request access/confirmation, correction, and use applicable objection/prevention and complaint rights. Contact **[privacy contact]** or the Personal Data Protection Office using [pdpo.go.ug](https://pdpo.go.ug/). Counsel must confirm response procedures, periods, available forms and any exceptions.

### 14. Breaches and notice

If you suspect unauthorized access, disclosure or loss of your account or trip information, contact **[security/privacy contact]** promptly. Fave will follow the approved incident plan, notify the Office immediately when required by regulation 33 and follow the Office's decision/direction about individual notice. This draft makes no promise that systems are risk-free.

### 15. Versioning and consent records

The app will show client terms and privacy notice during account setup, and booking/payment/cancellation/safety policies again before the relevant booking action. Each acceptance must record immutable document ID, version, language, content hash, account, context (`client_onboarding` or booking ID), timestamp and separate consent choice where applicable. New mandatory terms before payment require review and fresh acceptance. Do not treat a general terms acceptance as assignment-time contact-sharing consent.
