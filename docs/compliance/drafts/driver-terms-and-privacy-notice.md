### 3. Application and eligibility

To be considered, provide accurate information and the documents shown in the current Fave verification checklist. The checklist may request:

- confirmation that you are a Ugandan citizen; Fave's first release does not accept foreign-national or refugee driver applications;
- legal name and contact details;

Only an approved and currently eligible driver with a currently approved vehicle may be presented for a booking. You may not use another person's account or provide trips through another person's vehicle listing unless the approved arrangement expressly permits it.

### 4. Licence and vehicle obligations

You must hold and maintain all licences, permits, vehicle authorizations, inspection/fitness certificates and insurance required for your precise vehicle and transport activity. The final checklist and responsibility split will name each required document, issuing authority, validity period, renewal window, and the party responsible for obtaining it. **This draft does not assert which PSV/operator licence, roadworthiness certificate, insurance class, or vehicle marking applies to a Fave trip.** Do not accept trips while a required item is expired, suspended, withdrawn or under review.

You must tell Fave promptly about any change that could make your application, licence, insurance, vehicle facts or eligibility inaccurate or invalid. Fave may pause the listing while current information is verified. A Fave approval is a Platform decision, not a government licence, inspection certificate or insurance guarantee.

### 5. Trip acceptance, service and safety

The final agreement will state the trip scope, acceptance/cancellation process, passenger and luggage limits, agreed route/itinerary handling, price and payment, no-show process, and support contact **[complete after decisions D-05–D-07]**. Do not materially change the vehicle, driver, price or agreed service without the required client agreement and Platform workflow.

Follow applicable traffic, licensing, safety and insurance requirements. Use seat belts and other legally required safety equipment; do not drive while impaired or disqualified. Report collision, injury, harassment, crime, breakdown, vehicle substitution, lost property, or immediate safety concern through **[24-hour emergency/support contact]** as soon as practicable. For an immediate threat, contact the appropriate emergency service first, then Fave. Preserve relevant trip and incident details; do not publish a passenger's identity, image, itinerary or contact information.

No statement in this agreement removes a duty that the law places on you or Fave, or replaces practical safety controls. Counsel must define each party's reporting, insurance-notification and assistance responsibilities without a blanket waiver.

### 14. Retention

The current draft retention schedule is **not approved**. Regulation 22(2) of the Digital Networks Regulations says licensees retain customer data for at least five years from collection, while the Data Protection and Privacy Act limits retention to the relevant purpose/legal period and calls for secure deletion or de-identification at expiry. Counsel must classify each item in section 12, resolve the five-year rule and legal holds, and approve each period and deletion method before Fave activates any expiry or purge for these records. Backups and replicated copies must be included in the schedule.

You may complain to the Personal Data Protection Office (PDPO) through its current official channels at [pdpo.go.ug](https://pdpo.go.ug/). Counsel must confirm the required forms, response periods and any limits.

This notice does not promise that a breach cannot occur.
