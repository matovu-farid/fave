## Fresh primary-source check

Checked on 17 October 2026 (Kampala time):

1. [NITA-U laws index](https://www.nita.go.ug/laws-and-regulations) and its [Electronic Transactions Act, Chapter 99](https://www.nita.go.ug/sites/default/files/2026-09/Electronic%20Transactions%20Act%20cap%2099.pdf). Sections 4, 12–15 and 23 concern accessible incorporated terms, electronic contract review/acceptance and receipt, supplier particulars, full prices and transaction records. Section 15 supports receipt time rather than the later staff-reply time for cancellation instructions. Section 23(7)(j)'s specified-date transport exception disapplies **section 23(3)**; it does not supply a blanket exception to **section 24**. Section 24 provides its own seven-day consumer cancellation right and full refund within thirty days of cancellation. Section 26 addresses timely performance, notice of unavailability and refund of payments within thirty days; section 27 prevents contracting out of Part IV consumer rights. The copy preserves these rights rather than allowing a commercial deposit table to override them. Applicability to the actual transaction and the exact legal contract event still require qualified review; no contrary exemption is assumed.
2. [Uganda Police Force FAQ](https://upf.go.ug/faq/) lists **999 and 112** as emergency numbers. The safety policy distinguishes police emergency contact from Fave's non-continuous support and avoids claiming GPS monitoring or an SOS service.

Earlier primary research on capacity, reasonable compensation, data protection, insurance, retention and tax is retained with its dates in the [client-policy research record](client-policy-research-and-review.md). These new documents do not pretend those earlier checks are fresh regulatory confirmation.

Comparative structure checked: [Airbnb terms](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/2908), for showing applicable cancellation terms before booking and separating changes and service-failure remedies.
