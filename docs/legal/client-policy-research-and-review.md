## Counsel source and alignment note — not client-facing copy

Official sources checked on 16 October 2026:

- [Electronic Transactions Act, consolidated 2023](https://ulii.org/sw/akn/ug/act/2011/8/eng%402023-12-31), sections 23, 24, 26 and 27, supports complete supplier/transaction disclosures, reproducible records, the separate statutory cancellation right, service-unavailability refunds and preservation of consumer rights. The dated-transport exception disapplies section 23(3); it is not treated as a blanket exception to section 24. This consolidation's numbering differs from the [original 2011 Gazette](https://ulii.org/en/akn/ug/act/2011/8/eng%402011-03-18/publication). Compare provision titles and source versions rather than silently mixing numbers.
- [NITA-U: Data Protection and Privacy Act, Cap. 97](https://www.nita.go.ug/sites/default/files/2026-09/Data%20Protection%20and%20Privacy%20Act%20cap%2097.pdf), especially sections 7–9, 13–14, 18–19 and 24–28, addresses grounds, notices, minimization, retention/transfers and rights. Confirm section 9 treatment of financial/health information and required consent; ordinary contractual purpose alone does not resolve that separate restriction. No completed basis/transfer assessment is claimed.
- [Ministry of Works and Transport: Digital Networks Regulations, 2023](https://works.go.ug/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Traffic-and-Road-Safety-Digital-Networks-Regulations-2023.pdf), regulations 3, 17–18 and 22, concern licensing, registration/service agreements and licensee customer-data retention of at least five years from collection. Determine applicability and record categories before approving retention; this is not a decision to retain every raw file for five years.
- [Personal Data Protection Office](https://pdpo.go.ug/) is the complaint route. The legal reviewer also checked [Data Protection and Privacy Regulations 2021](https://pdpo.go.ug/media/2022/03/Data_Protection_and_Privacy_Regulations-2021.pdf), regulation 30, for overseas/onward-processing conditions. Fave must verify provider safeguards and applicable consent or regulatory demonstration; the notice supplies no blanket transfer permission.
- [Contracts Act](https://ulii.org/en/akn/ug/act/2010/7/eng%402023-12-31), sections 10 and 61, informs booking capacity and reasonable compensation for stipulated cancellation amounts. The copy requires adult authorized bookers and preserves review of unreasonable penalties.
- [Tax Procedures Code, consolidated July 2025](https://ulii.org/en/akn/ug/act/2014/14/eng%402025-07-01), section 15, informs the five-year tax-period-based minimum and proceedings exceptions. The six-year transaction-evidence policy is a separate choice informed by ordinary contract claims under the [Limitation Act](https://ulii.org/en/akn/ug/act/ord/1958/46/eng%402023-12-31), not a claim that every raw location or log must be retained for six years.
- [Motor Vehicle Insurance (Third Party Risks) Act](https://ulii.org/en/akn/ug/act/statute/1988/11/eng%402023-12-31), section 14, informed preserving passenger negligence/wilful-default death and bodily-injury protection. Insurance is not described as an exclusive remedy or a discharge of Fave's own duties.
