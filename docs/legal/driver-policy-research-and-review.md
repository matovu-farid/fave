## Retention choices and implementation gap

The notice distinguishes a **Fave policy choice** of five years for minimal provider registration/accountability records from any confirmed applicable statutory customer-data minimum. It separately sets a twelve-month collection period for unapproved raw evidence, necessity-reviewed current approved evidence and ninety-day removal after verified replacement or no further need following the relationship/listing ending. Necessary decision results remain under their own period. Supporting data has at least a ninety-day access opportunity after communication of the decision even if ordinary expiry would be earlier; this is not a complaints limitation. Non-required received technical information has a ninety-day fallback and non-required inbox/delivery records a twelve-month fallback; statutory/transaction requirements take priority. Transaction/tax evidence follows the six-year/later tax-period rule; identified lawful holds and required decision access take priority. These are finished wording choices for exact-version review, not approved category mapping, an activated TTL or proof of deletion tooling.

The concrete resubmission/purge bug identified during review is repaired in the [17 October retention implementation](../superpowers/plans/2026-10-17-private-evidence-retention.md): prior file collection/deadline/policy snapshots survive resubmission, only each file's own deadline controls retirement, and a transactional durable cleanup job separates database retirement from retryable R2 deletion. Stale application redaction cannot erase a replacement submission, and retained old evidence remains ineligible for current approval. This is not full notice/retention alignment: the schema/purge still needs the per-category necessity, decision-access, lawful-hold and backup lifecycle, plus approved category mapping. A changed `retention_days` value alone cannot supply those controls. Keep activation blocked until the remaining work is reconciled and tested.

No automatic export/deletion or secure incident-upload feature is promised. Manual rights handling, statutory response times, recipient corrections, backup removal and secure support evidence still require operational verification. Review membership/access and field-encryption code provide evidence for the specific access descriptions; they do not prove a complete privacy compliance program.
