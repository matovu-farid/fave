## 1. Your trip with Fave

Fave undertakes to provide the trip you book with us through the agreed car and assigned driver. Using a driver or another service provider does not remove Fave's own contractual or legal responsibilities to you.

The service supports eligible pickups in Kampala, Wakiso and Mukono, destinations within Uganda, and trips lasting up to seven days. Coverage is checked using your pickup address or map pin. Trip dates, quote deadlines and cancellation times use Kampala time.

You must be at least 18 and legally able to enter the agreement to hold a booking account. If booking for an organization or other passengers, you must have the necessary authority. Give accurate trip and contact details. Tell Fave before accepting the quote about children, accessibility needs, luggage or a time-critical connection that affects the arrangements. Share only the information needed to plan the trip safely.

## 2. Review the estimate first

Enter your pickup, destination, dates, passenger and luggage needs, then choose an eligible car. Fave shows a saved, itemized estimate before you decide whether to ask to book. Review and correct these details before continuing.

The selected car's transport rate comes from its driver's Kampala–Jinja round-trip benchmark price and Fave's approved reference road-distance basis. The benchmark combines the car's operating costs, including fuel, and its type and comfort. There is no universal per-kilometre rate used in place of an approved rate for that car.

The estimate shows **outbound transport** and **return transport** separately. Fave calculates the actual driving routes in each direction separately. The return line covers the car's return from the agreed destination to the trip's pickup point, whether or not you travel on that leg; the itinerary states the passenger arrangements. A different return destination or an unagreed detour is not silently added to the price.

**Driver lodging** is a separate line based on the assigned driver's registered nightly rate and the number of nights between the start and return dates in Kampala time. The start date is included and the return date excluded: Monday to Tuesday is one night. Lodging may be any suitable type. **Fave does not arrange or pay for accommodation for you or your passengers.**

The estimate retains the rates, distances, dates and calculation used. The quote identifies its road-distance basis, inclusions, exclusions, taxes, fees, currency and rounding. A route or travel-time estimate helps with planning; it does not guarantee traffic, road conditions or journey duration.

## 3. Ask staff to check availability

When you ask to book the selected car for your dates, Fave creates a staff request linked to your saved estimate. Staff contact the assigned driver to check both that driver and car for those dates. You do not pay at this stage.

A pending check does not reserve the car or driver and has no automatic acceptance or expiry. An unanswered call stays awaiting follow-up. The app shows the status and lets you withdraw the request. Staff update it as checks are completed; there is no guaranteed confirmation time.

If the car or driver is unavailable, choose another car and review its estimate. Fave does not silently substitute a different car or driver. An availability confirmation covers only the requested dates.

## 4. Review the final quote and holds

After staff confirm availability, Fave issues the final quote at the **same total and itemized price as your saved estimate**. A later change to a car or driver rate does not increase that saved price. Fave holds the selected car and driver for your dates for **24 hours from staff confirmation** while you decide. The app shows the deadline.

Before proceeding, review the itinerary, car, dates, complete total, deposit, remaining balance, payment due events, quote deadline and the current booking, payment, cancellation and safety disclosures. You can correct the request or decide not to continue. An itinerary change before booking requires a new estimate and availability check, rather than an undisclosed edit to your saved price.

The deposit step begins only after staff confirmation, your acceptance of the current final quote and disclosures, and your separate choice to share your phone number with the assigned driver for the trip. The current flow requires this contact-sharing choice to proceed. A phone number is a contact detail; phone OTP verification is not required in this version. Contact sharing and your privacy rights are explained in the [client terms and privacy notice](client-terms-and-privacy-notice.md).

Starting deposit checkout replaces the quote hold with a **30-minute hold from checkout start** for that car and driver. It is a separate checkout window; the app shows its deadline. On expiry or confirmed payment failure, the hold is released. If a quote or checkout hold expires, Fave must check availability again before you continue. Payment is blocked while staff review a change in driver availability or eligibility.

## 5. When your booking becomes confirmed

Your acceptance submits the booking request to Fave. Subject to mandatory legal rules, your trip contract is formed when **you receive Fave's booking confirmation**, sent after you accept the current final quote and disclosures and your deposit is verified. An estimate, request, staff availability check, temporary hold, payment screenshot or bank debit alone is not confirmation of the trip.

If a payment is pending, ask support to check its provider reference before paying again. If payment is received but Fave cannot confirm the agreed booking, Fave must refund it. If Fave cannot provide the accepted car and driver after payment, you receive the full deposit and return of any other payment for the unavailable service, without having to accept an undisclosed substitute. These refunds do not exclude any additional remedy available under law.

Booking-scoped contact release follows verified deposit and the required booking checks. Before then, you do not receive the driver's private identity or contact details. Use released contact details only to arrange and carry out the trip.

## 6. Changes after booking

Ask Fave before changing a route, dates or other agreed arrangements. A change takes effect only after Fave confirms availability where required, shows you the revised itinerary and price, and you accept the amendment. Fave retains the original accepted quote and the amendment. A later rate change or an unaccepted cost difference does not automatically authorize a surcharge.

An extension requires confirmation that the agreed car and driver are available for the added dates. Added driver-lodging nights use the nightly rate saved in your accepted quote. Fave shows the added nights and revised total before you accept; payment for those added nights is due immediately when you accept the extension. Other route changes or charges also require a disclosed, accepted amendment. An extension is not guaranteed or necessarily available through a self-service app control.

For cancellation, missed pickup, delay, interruption or a service problem, use the [cancellation policy](client-cancellation-policy.md) and contact support. A no-show label does not automatically authorize forfeiture or an additional charge. Nothing in a change or cancellation process removes your mandatory rights.

## 7. Records and support

Fave keeps your saved estimate, accepted quote, calculation, applicable document versions, acceptance records, booking confirmation and payment records. We provide the applicable terms and confirmation in a form you can save. Request a readable electronic copy from [farid@fidexa.org](mailto:farid@fidexa.org), using your account or booking reference, during the applicable retention period in the privacy notice. Account restrictions do not remove this request route.

For a question or complaint, email that address with your booking reference and a clear explanation. Do not send payment credentials or unnecessary identity documents. Support email is not continuously monitored and is not an emergency dispatch service. You may use any court, regulator or complaint route available under applicable law; contacting Fave does not waive those rights.
