## 1. Who you pay and what you authorize

You pay Fave for the agreed trip. Fave undertakes to provide that trip through the agreed car and assigned driver and remains responsible for its own contractual and legal duties. A payment provider processes the payment; its role does not replace Fave's obligations to you.

Pay only through the booking payment flow and methods actually offered for your transaction. Fave identifies the payment provider and relevant payment terms before you authorize a payment. Only methods enabled and displayed for that transaction are offered; a description of mobile money or cards does not promise that every method is available for every payment.

You authorize only the amount and purpose shown. This policy does not authorize undisclosed charges, unrestricted access to a payment account, automatic penalties or a further payment merely because someone claims to represent Fave. Ask support to verify any payment request that does not match your accepted quote or amendment.

## 2. Your complete price

Prices are in **Uganda shillings (UGX)**. The quote shows the outbound transport, return transport, separate driver lodging and any other expressly agreed component. It identifies all applicable taxes, fees, inclusions and exclusions before you commit. **Payment-processing fees are included in the quoted total. Fave adds no separate processing fee at checkout.**

The car's transport rate uses its driver's one Kampala–Jinja round-trip benchmark price and the approved reference road-distance basis. Fave prices your outbound and return driving routes separately using that car's rate. The return charge covers the car's return to the agreed pickup point whether or not you ride back; your itinerary states the passenger arrangements. There is no universal per-kilometre fallback for a car without an approved rate.

Driver lodging uses the assigned driver's registered nightly rate multiplied by the number of nights between the start and return dates in Kampala time, including the start date and excluding the return date. Monday to Tuesday is one night. **This component is for the driver's lodging only. Fave does not arrange or pay for client or passenger accommodation.**

Each monetary line and the deposit are rounded once to the nearest whole shilling; a positive half shilling rounds up. The total adds the rounded line amounts. The balance is the total minus the deposit. A displayed whole-shilling per-kilometre rate may be approximate; Fave retains the precise rate and calculation used for your quote.

The saved estimate and final quote retain their rates, dates, distances, night count and calculation. Fave retains the disclosure versions you accept with your booking acceptance record. After staff confirm availability, the final quote has the same total and itemized price as the saved estimate. A later rate change does not silently increase your booking.

## 3. Deposit, balance and confirmation

Before you commit, your quote states the exact **deposit, remaining balance and the events or dates when each payment becomes due**. Those disclosed, accepted terms determine your payment schedule. This policy does not set a universal deposit percentage or permit Fave to invent a new due date after acceptance.

No trip payment is requested while staff check availability. After staff confirmation, you review and accept the final quote and current disclosures and make the separate required contact-sharing choice before starting deposit checkout. The date-specific car and driver hold lasts 24 hours from staff confirmation. Checkout replaces that hold with a 30-minute hold from checkout start. The app shows each applicable deadline; expiry requires another availability check before continuing. See the [booking policy](client-booking-policy.md).

Fave verifies the deposit with the provider before recording it as received. Your acceptance submits the booking request to Fave. Subject to mandatory legal rules, your trip contract is formed when you receive Fave's booking confirmation, sent after your acceptance of the current final quote and disclosures and verification of the deposit. A provider redirect, mobile-money approval prompt, screenshot or bank debit alone does not confirm the trip. If payment is received but Fave cannot confirm the agreed booking, it must be refunded.

The deposit and balance are separate payment transactions. Before you pay a balance, Fave shows the amount and its basis under your accepted schedule. Fave does not automatically charge a disputed balance or treat silence as agreement to an unaccepted amount or disputed completion. Report a concern through support so Fave can explain the calculation, review the service and evidence, and record the outcome. This policy creates no automatic completion deadline, forfeiture or extra charge.

## 4. Pending, failed and duplicate payments

Payment is received only when Fave verifies the transaction with the provider and records its successful status. Fave records the booking, amount, currency, payment stage, provider reference, status and timestamp, and supplies a receipt or payment record.

If a payment is pending, **do not pay again merely because the app has not updated**. Contact support with the booking and provider reference so it can check the outcome. A failed or expired payment does not extend an expired car and driver hold. Fave explains the next step and any required availability check before a fresh attempt. A late or duplicate payment does not itself create another booking or authorize Fave to keep an amount that is not owed.

Never send a card PIN, mobile-money PIN, password or authentication code to Fave by email, chat, phone or a support form. Enter authentication details only in the payment method's secure prompt. Fave does not need those secrets to investigate a transaction.

## 5. Changes and added nights

Fave does not silently increase an accepted total. A change requires a disclosed amendment and your acceptance before it is applied or charged. Fave records the original quote, revision and acceptance.

For an extension, Fave first checks availability for the agreed car and driver. Added driver-lodging nights use the nightly rate saved in your accepted quote. Fave shows the amended dates, added nights, calculation and revised total before you accept. Payment for those added nights is due immediately when you accept the extension. Other route changes or charges require their own disclosed, accepted amendment.

## 6. Refunds and payment disputes

The [cancellation policy](client-cancellation-policy.md) explains the commercial deposit schedule and statutory cancellation and refund rights. If Fave cannot provide the accepted car and driver after payment, Fave refunds the full deposit and returns any other payment for the unavailable service. You do not have to accept an undisclosed substitute. A deposit-only refund is insufficient where the law requires return of all payments.

Fave returns refunds without undue delay and within any mandatory deadline. For a covered statutory cancellation, the full refund is due within thirty days of cancellation. If the agreed service is unavailable, Fave must notify you before the agreed service time expires and return all payments for that service within the applicable thirty-day statutory period. Any shorter mandatory deadline applies. Provider posting times, settlement holds or recovery from a driver do not extend Fave's legal deadline or reduce the refund owed. Fave does not deduct a provider fee from a statutory full refund.

Fave explains the refund amount, basis, route and status. It uses the original payment route where supported. If that route cannot receive the refund, Fave contacts you to agree a lawful alternative, without shifting provider recovery risk to you. A refund is not described as completed merely because it has been requested.

For an incorrect, duplicate or unrecognized charge, email [farid@fidexa.org](mailto:farid@fidexa.org) with your booking or receipt reference and the amount concerned. Do not send credentials or full card details. Contacting Fave does not prevent you from contacting your bank, card issuer, mobile-money operator, regulator or other available complaint body.

## 7. Driver payment and your records

Fave pays the driver's itemized full pay for each trip day from Fave's business funds that morning, including that day's driver-lodging component. This is not an extra accommodation bill for you. Fave's driver settlement does not remove any refund or other remedy owed to you, including for an unused service.

Fave retains accepted quotes, revisions, calculations, applicable document versions, acceptances, payment references and statuses, and refund or dispute decisions. The privacy notice explains retention, including at least six years for transaction records and longer where tax duties or a lawful hold require it. Request a readable electronic copy using your account or booking reference through [farid@fidexa.org](mailto:farid@fidexa.org). Account restrictions do not remove that request route. Support email is not continuously monitored; applicable legal duties still apply.
