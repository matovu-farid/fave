## 1. Immediate danger

**For an emergency in Uganda, call Police on 999 or 112.** These are the emergency numbers published by the [Uganda Police Force](https://upf.go.ug/faq/). Give your location and describe what has happened. Follow the emergency services' instructions and move away from immediate danger where it is safe to do so.

Fave support is for trip assistance and reporting concerns. Email, phone support and app notifications are **not an emergency dispatch service or a promise of continuous monitoring**. A notification or read marker is not confirmation that a safety report has been seen or help is on its way. This version does not provide continuous GPS tracking. Contact emergency services directly when urgent help is needed; do not wait for an app or email reply.

## 2. Before travel

Review your pickup, dates, destination, passenger and luggage arrangements. Before boarding, compare the arriving car and driver with the assignment details Fave has provided for your booking. If they do not match, do not board until Fave has checked and resolved the difference. Fave does not silently substitute a different car or driver.

Tell Fave before accepting the quote about child passengers, necessary accessibility arrangements, luggage or another requirement that affects safe travel. Give the practical information needed to arrange the trip; do not send unnecessary diagnoses, medical records or identity documents. Fave must consider requests fairly and explain what it can safely and lawfully provide before you commit. A disability or a request for assistance is not itself misconduct or a waiver of rights.

A child may travel only with the necessary parent or guardian authorization and lawful supervision and restraints. Agree the required supervision, suitable child restraints and how they will be provided before confirming arrangements. Do not travel without a legally required safety arrangement. Respect the car's lawful seating and luggage capacity; never overload the vehicle or use a seat without a required restraint.

An approved listing means it has passed Fave's applicable review process. It is not government approval, a guarantee against harm or proof that every possible check has been performed. Fave remains responsible for its own trip-performance promise and legal duties. Reviews do not replace required licences, insurance, vehicle fitness or operational safeguards.

## 3. During the trip

Use the required restraints and follow lawful, reasonable safety instructions. Treat passengers, drivers and staff respectfully. Do not threaten, harass, discriminate against or deliberately endanger another person. Do not ask a driver to speed, break road rules, drive while impaired or continue when fatigue or conditions make driving unsafe.

Allow necessary safe stops and rest breaks. Raise an unsafe route, vehicle fault or other concern as soon as it is safe to do so. A schedule or connection does not require unsafe driving. Discuss itinerary changes with Fave; do not pressure a driver into an unagreed or unsafe detour. The [booking policy](client-booking-policy.md) explains accepted amendments and extensions.

Driver lodging is a separate quoted component for the driver's needs. It does not include accommodation for clients or passengers. Fave's obligations to respond reasonably to a trip disruption still apply.

## 4. Breakdown, incident or interruption

For a collision, breakdown, harassment, unsafe driving or another incident, prioritize immediate safety and emergency assistance. Contact Fave when safe, using your booking reference, location where relevant, and a factual description. Fave must take reasonable steps within its control to limit disruption and arrange a safe, lawful response. Suspending an unsafe journey or restricting an account does not authorize abandoning a passenger in an unsafe situation.

Any proposed replacement car or driver must be explained and agreed; an undisclosed substitute is not automatically accepted. Fave must address any service it cannot provide under the [cancellation and refund policy](client-cancellation-policy.md). Reporting a concern, a necessary safety pause or a no-show label does not by itself authorize forfeiture, an extra charge or collection of a disputed balance. Events beyond reasonable control do not excuse Fave's own fault or remove mandatory remedies.

## 5. Contact, evidence and privacy

Use booking contact details only to arrange and carry out that trip. Do not publish or reuse another person's phone number, private identity documents or detailed itinerary for unrelated purposes. For an incident report, send only necessary information; ask support how to provide sensitive evidence securely. Do not put identity documents or other people's private information in a public post. This does not prevent a lawful report to police, a regulator, a court or another appropriate complaint body.

Fave staff and drivers do not need your card PIN, mobile-money PIN, password or authentication code. Do not send these secrets by phone, chat or email. Enter payment authentication only through the payment method's secure prompt. Verify an unexpected payment request with Fave before paying.

Fave's [privacy notice](client-terms-and-privacy-notice.md#part-b--client-privacy-notice) explains the handling of account, trip and incident information. This policy does not promise an in-app emergency button, an incident-upload feature or continuous monitoring.

## 6. Responsibility and complaints

Fave must perform its own obligations with reasonable care and skill. This policy does not exclude liability for death or injury caused by negligence, fraud, deliberate wrongdoing or any liability or consumer right that cannot lawfully be limited. It creates no blanket client indemnity, automatic damage deduction or fixed monetary liability cap. The responsibility provisions in the client terms remain subject to those protections.

Email [farid@fidexa.org](mailto:farid@fidexa.org) with your booking reference and the concern you want reviewed. Fave must explain the basis of a decision and any claimed charge and allow a fair opportunity to dispute it. There is no compulsory internal complaint deadline or requirement to give up access to a court, regulator, police or another lawful complaint route.
