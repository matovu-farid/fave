### 2. Seeing a price before asking to book

Enter your pickup, destination, dates, passenger and luggage needs, then choose an eligible car. Fave shows a saved, itemized estimate before you decide whether to ask to book it. Check and correct the details before continuing.

The car's transport price reflects its operating costs, including fuel, and its type and comfort. Fave derives that car's rate from the driver's submitted Kampala–Jinja round-trip benchmark and an approved reference road distance. Your estimate shows **outbound transport** and **return transport** separately, calculated from the driving-route distances for your trip. There is no universal per-kilometre rate that substitutes for an unapproved car rate.

The return line covers the car's return from the agreed destination to the trip's pickup point. It applies whether or not you ride on that return leg; your accepted itinerary states the passenger arrangements. The quote identifies the road-distance basis, currency and rounding for each leg. An unagreed detour or different return destination is not silently added to the price or treated as included.

Driver lodging is a separate line. It uses the applicable driver's registered nightly rate and the number of nights between your start and return dates in Kampala time. The start date is included and the return date excluded: Monday to Tuesday is one night. The estimate retains the inputs, rates and calculation. **Driver lodging is for the driver only. Fave does not arrange or pay for accommodation for you or your passengers.** Lodging may be any suitable type, not only a hotel.

A map route, distance or travel-time estimate supports planning. It does not guarantee traffic, road conditions or journey duration. The quote must identify its inclusions, exclusions, taxes and fees before you accept; a cost not disclosed in the accepted quote is not automatically authorized by these terms.

### 3. Requesting an availability check

When you ask to book that car for your dates, Fave creates a staff availability request linked to your saved estimate. Staff contact the assigned driver to check that both the driver and car are available for those dates. You do not pay for the trip at this stage.

While staff are checking, the app shows the request's status and lets you withdraw it. A pending request does not reserve the car or driver. An unanswered call stays awaiting follow-up; it does not automatically become an accepted booking. We will update the request as staff complete their checks; a specific confirmation time is not guaranteed.

If unavailable, Fave asks you to choose another car. Fave does not silently substitute a different car or driver. Confirmation applies only to your requested dates and does not make the car available for every other date.

### 4. Final quote, temporary hold and payment

After staff confirm availability, Fave issues the final quote at the **same total and itemized price as your saved estimate**. A later car or driver rate change does not increase that saved estimate when it becomes the final quote. The car and driver are held for your requested dates for 24 hours from staff confirmation while you decide whether to book at that saved price. The app shows the quote deadline.

Review the trip, car, dates, total, deposit, balance, expiry and current booking, payment, cancellation and safety disclosures. You can correct a request or decide not to continue. Changing the itinerary before booking requires a new estimate and availability check, rather than an undisclosed edit to the saved price.

The deposit step begins only after staff confirmation, your acceptance of the final quote and current disclosures, and your separate contact-sharing choice. When you start deposit checkout, the quote hold is replaced by a 30-minute hold for the car and driver, measured from checkout start; the app shows its deadline. If the quote or hold expires, availability must be checked again before continuing. If the driver's availability or eligibility changes, payment is blocked while staff review it.

A request, estimate and temporary hold are not a confirmed booking. Your trip contract with Fave is formed when Fave issues the booking confirmation after you accept the current final quote and disclosures and your deposit is verified. Before that confirmation, do not treat an estimate, staff availability check, payment screenshot or bank debit alone as a confirmed trip. If payment is received but Fave cannot confirm the agreed booking, it must be refunded; this does not limit any other right you have under law.

Payment-processing fees are included in the quoted total; Fave does not add a separate processing fee at checkout. Before payment, your quote shows the complete total, deposit, remaining balance, due dates and applicable fees. Pay only through the payment methods actually offered at checkout.
