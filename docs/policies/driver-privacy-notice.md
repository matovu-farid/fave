## 3. Lawful purposes and choices

Necessary application, account and service processing supports steps you request and performance of the driver agreement. Collection and retention required or authorized by transport, tax or other applicable law serve those specified duties. We limit each use to relevant information; a contractual purpose does not override a separate statutory restriction.

Where consent is required, including for specially protected financial or health information, Fave must obtain the specific, informed consent required by law or establish the particular lawful exception before processing. Agreement acceptance is not a substitute. We do not request routine medical histories or diagnoses. You can withdraw consent for a consent-based use; Fave must explain the effect on the requested service and any distinct lawful retention duty, without treating withdrawal as a retrospective waiver or penalty.

We use software to check required documents, accepted versions, eligibility, evidence expiry, dates and conflicting holds. These checks may block an action. Designated staff review the application and decide approval, correction, rejection or suspension. If a result is wrong or significantly affects you, request an explanation and human review through the contact above. This does not allow staff to bypass a legal or safety requirement or promise an unbuilt in-app appeal control.

You are not enrolled in marketing by applying. A future marketing use needs its own lawful notice and choice. Fave does not sell your personal information under this service design. Change push permission in device settings and check the inbox for current trip updates; notifications are not continuous monitoring or emergency dispatch.

## 4. Access and disclosure

- **Private identity reviewer:** currently **Matovu Nkoba Farid** is the only designated person permitted to open drivers' private ID evidence for Fave verification. Sensitive evidence access and decisions are restricted, require the review unlock and are audited. A routine operations role does not itself grant this access. - **Other authorized Fave operations staff:** only the account, vehicle, date, contact, status and support information necessary for their duties. A staff availability call can use the driver's registered contact without exposing a client's private details in that queue.
- **Clients and the public:** public listings show approved vehicle facts and approved photos. Your private ID, portrait evidence, registration/insurance documents, private addresses and payout details are not catalogue information. Only your necessary assignment name/contact details are released to the client after verified deposit and the required booking checks, for that booking. Paying a deposit never releases your National ID number or copies, private verification portrait, or private vehicle evidence to the client.
- **Service providers:** Cloudflare supports hosting/storage and delivery infrastructure; Google supports map/address/route functions; your selected Google or Apple sign-in provider authenticates you; Expo and the relevant mobile push service deliver enabled notifications. Each receives information necessary for its function, with applicable safeguards. A selected payment provider receives only the relevant payment/payout information after its role is explained. No particular payment provider is promised by this notice.
- **Authorities and professional advisers:** relevant information only where a legal duty or justified investigation, claim or safety response permits disclosure. These categories do not permit unrestricted access to private evidence or credentials.

Fave must document the appropriate role and protection for each recipient. Some infrastructure, sign-in, map and notification processing may take place outside Uganda. Fave must establish the lawful transfer condition and required protection before that processing, including applicable onward-transfer conditions. Your acknowledgement is not blanket consent to unspecified countries or recipients. Ask [farid@fidexa.org](mailto:farid@fidexa.org) for the current locations and safeguards relevant to your information. A provider's own notice does not remove Fave's duties.

## 5. Security and device information

Fave uses authenticated, server-authorized access, restricted private evidence storage and audit records for sensitive access and decisions. Application handling protects the stored National ID number separately from ordinary profile fields. Private evidence is not served as public listing media. These controls do not guarantee that a security incident can never occur.

The web service uses essential sign-in cookies and the app uses protected device storage for session information. Your selected sign-in provider may have its own session. Signing out of Fave and closing or clearing a shared browser/device are separate actions. Do not share your account or leave private documents visible on a shared screen. Report suspected misuse or disclosure to [farid@fidexa.org](mailto:farid@fidexa.org). Do not attach extra ID copies to an ordinary email; ask for a justified secure route where evidence is needed. Fave must contain and investigate a breach, follow the applicable immediate regulatory-notification duty and any required direction about informing affected people. Support is not continuously monitored; for immediate danger contact emergency services directly.

## 6. Retention and deletion

These periods govern Fave's use of records, including rejected or inactive applications. Retention is not permission to keep an account active, deliver notifications after opt-out or continue unrelated processing.

| Record | Period or deletion event |
| --- | --- |
| Minimal provider registration/contact information, citizenship affirmation, checklist results, submission history and review outcome | Fave's provider-accountability period is five years from collection. Current information necessary to operate an active approved profile remains for the later of that period and the end of the driver relationship. This policy period is distinct from the legal customer-data minimum discussed below and does not automatically apply to all raw images. |
| Raw National ID/portrait and driver-checklist images for an application that has not been approved, including rejected or withdrawn applications | Twelve months from each item's collection, unless a specific legal duty or identified hold requires it longer. Rejection, withdrawal or another field update does not restart that period. If a pending check needs current evidence beyond that period, Fave must explain the need and request an appropriate update; it cannot silently keep the old image indefinitely. |
| Current raw National ID/portrait and driver-checklist evidence for an approved driver | Only while necessary to verify current eligibility, with necessity reviewed at least annually. Remove within ninety days after a verified replacement or when no longer necessary following the end of the driver relationship, unless an applicable legal minimum or identified hold requires it longer. Keep the necessary non-image verification/decision record under its own period. |
| Private vehicle registration, authority-to-use, insurance/fitness evidence and listing-review records | Minimal registration and review-result records follow the five-year provider-accountability period. Unapproved raw evidence follows the twelve-month collection period; approved current raw evidence follows the necessity review and ninety-day removal rule above when replaced or no longer needed after delisting. A confirmed applicable legal minimum or identified hold takes priority. |
| Public approved vehicle photos and public listing display | Remove from public display when the listing is delisted, approval ends or the image is no longer authorized. Any copy lawfully needed as transport, review or claim evidence remains private for that category's period, rather than remaining publicly visible. |
| Accepted work, trip/price calculations, daily-pay and tax/payment records, accepted agreements and related evidence | Six years after the trip, settlement or cancellation is closed, or five years after the relevant tax period ends if that expires later. Retain necessary transaction evidence, not every raw ID image merely because a trip occurred. |
| Support, complaints and incident/decision audit records | Five years from collection; identified evidence for a disputed trip, settlement or legal claim follows its six-year period after resolution where necessary. |
| Received driver-originating account/session/device information, including collected IP/browser details | Five years from collection where covered by the transport record requirement, or the associated transaction/dispute period where needed as evidence. Where neither applies, ninety days from collection. Restrict inactive historical records from ordinary operational use. |
| Inbox and delivery records | Five years from collection where covered by the transport record requirement, or the associated transaction/dispute period where needed as evidence. Where neither applies, twelve months from collection. This does not extend active push-token use after permission ends. |
| Usable authentication credentials and push tokens | Revoke or remove from active use when the session, account permission or push registration ends. Keep only a restricted inactive historical record if separately required by law; retention never reactivates a revoked credential or delivery permission. |
| Additional server-generated diagnostic logs outside required transport/account records | Ninety days, unless identified entries are needed for a specific lawful incident or hold. This shorter period does not override a mandatory record requirement. |

The Digital Networks Regulations require a licensee to retain data collected from its customers for at least five years from collection. Fave must establish which driver/provider categories fall within that requirement before applying it; the wording is not an automatic five-year rule for every rejected applicant or raw ID image. Where that legal minimum applies, it takes priority over a shorter period above for the identified record. Fave's separate provider-accountability and raw-evidence periods are policy choices, not a claim that the law names those periods for every driver record. Fave must classify the record, minimize excess data and remove information it has no lawful authority to retain. Additional, unsolicited sensitive information is not kept merely because an application exists. A legal requirement, court/regulatory instruction or necessary identified claim can require a different period; Fave must record the basis and explain the relevant exception when responding to a request.

For data used to make a decision about you, the ordinary expiry must leave at least ninety days after Fave communicates the decision for you to request its supporting information, or any longer access opportunity required by law. This is a record-access safeguard, not a deadline to complain or exercise other rights. A pending access, correction or dispute request prevents routine destruction of the identified evidence until the request and any required access period are resolved. A narrowly scoped legal hold identifies the records and reason and is reviewed at least every ninety days. Once it ends, records whose normal period has expired must be removed. At expiry, Fave securely deletes or irreversibly de-identifies the relevant record. Residual identifiable backup copies are isolated from routine use and expire within ninety days of primary removal, or sooner where law or a binding order requires, unless a specific lawful hold applies. Full deletion is not complete while such a copy remains; a restoration must reapply deletion restrictions.

The periods do not mean automated deletion or export is available in the current app. Use the request route below. Closing a profile or withdrawing from new work does not erase required trip/pay evidence; signing out is not a deletion request.

## 7. Your rights and requests

Subject to applicable law, ask whether we hold your information, request access or a readable copy, correct inaccurate information, request deletion of data no longer lawfully retained, and exercise applicable objection and automated-decision rights. You may withdraw consent for a use that relies on consent. Fave must explain any lawful refusal or retention exception and the response or review route.

Email [farid@fidexa.org](mailto:farid@fidexa.org) with your account or application reference and the request. Proportionate identity checks may be necessary before disclosure or changes. Do not send an ID number, document image, password or authentication code unless a justified secure process has been explained. A response must protect other people's information and meet applicable statutory timescales; ordinary support availability does not extend them.

You may complain directly to Uganda's [Personal Data Protection Office](https://pdpo.go.ug/) or use another court, regulator or remedy available under law. There is no compulsory internal complaint deadline or waiver of those routes.
