## 1. Our respective roles

Fave promises the client that Fave will provide the accepted trip through the assigned driver and car. You perform the driving and other expressly agreed trip tasks for Fave. A passenger does not become solely your customer because you drive the car, and using a driver does not remove Fave's own contractual, safety, licensing or other legal responsibilities.

This agreement does not decide your employment or other legal status by a label. Any mandatory employment, worker, tax, social-security, safety or other rights and duties arising from the actual relationship continue to apply. No clause requires you to waive them. A specific work arrangement must be lawful and consistent with those rights.

Registration does not guarantee a minimum number of trips, earnings or continuous work. You may tell Fave that you or your car are unavailable. Confirmed commitments, safe handling of an existing trip and accrued legal rights must still be addressed under this agreement.

## 2. Applying and keeping information current

You must be at least 18 and legally able to undertake the agreed work. The first release accepts driver applications from Ugandan citizens with a National ID. This is Fave's current application policy, not a statement that Ugandan law generally prohibits other people from driving or working.

Provide your accurate legal name, contact phone, nightly lodging rate, National ID number, clear front and back National ID images, and a current passport-style portrait. The portrait is a photograph of you; it is not a request for a passport document. Provide the additional driving and vehicle evidence identified in the applicable verification checklist. Do not falsify, alter or submit someone else's evidence. If a requirement is unclear, ask Fave before supplying unnecessary documents.

Signing in through Google or Apple identifies the account used for your application; it does not verify your National ID, driving entitlement or vehicle. Your phone is a contact detail; this release does not require phone-code verification. Secure your account and report suspected misuse. Do not share account access or let another person take your assigned trip.

Driver and vehicle applications have separate reviews. Fave may request corrections or current evidence and must give an understandable applicant-facing reason for a correction or refusal, subject to lawful security restrictions. Approval requires the applicable checks and current evidence; it is not government approval or a guarantee against harm. The [privacy notice](driver-privacy-notice.md) explains private evidence access. The current designated private identity reviewer is **Matovu Nkoba Farid**; an ordinary staff or client account does not have that access.

Tell Fave promptly about changed identity/contact details, a licence restriction, an incident affecting eligibility, lost vehicle authority, insurance changes, or other material inaccuracies. Do not provide a trip while a legally required authorization is absent, expired, suspended or invalid. Fave must also fulfil the obligations the law places on Fave; application approval is not a substitute for either party's compliance.

## 3. Cars, rates and agreed pay

You may submit more than one car for separate approval. You must own the car or have the authority needed to list and use it for the agreed service. Supply accurate vehicle facts, lawful passenger/luggage capacity and the required registration, authorization, fitness and insurance evidence. Maintain the car safely and disclose material changes before further work. Use current photos of the actual car and avoid exposing bystanders, number plates or private locations. Only car photos individually approved for client display may be published; private identity and vehicle documents are not listing photos.

For each car, supply **one Kampala–Jinja round-trip transport price**, covering that car's operating costs, including fuel, and its type and comfort. This is not a one-way price. Fave derives that car's rate using the approved reference road distance and calculates the client's outbound and return driving-route charges separately. There is no universal substitute rate. Your submitted benchmark is a pricing input; it is not by itself an agreed daily wage, commission or promise that you receive the whole client fare.

You separately set your **nightly lodging rate** during registration. It applies across destinations and covers your lodging needs, which need not mean a hotel. The trip-night count uses Kampala calendar dates, including the start date and excluding the return date: Monday to Tuesday is one night. Fave does not arrange or pay for client or passenger accommodation.

Before you agree to work a trip, Fave and you must agree its itinerary, dates, assigned car, tasks, itemized daily pay, lodging allocation, any agreed expenses, tax treatment, lawful deductions and payout method. Those arrangements must be recorded and available to you. No trip may proceed on the assumption that an undisclosed commission, deduction or expense allowance will be decided later. The client price and your pay are distinct records. Fave cannot reduce your agreed pay merely because its own margin, provider fee or client collection differs from its estimate.

## 4. Availability, holds and changes

A client first sees a saved estimate. If the client asks to book, Fave staff contact you to check the specified car and your availability for the requested dates. Give an accurate response and tell staff about conflicting commitments or conditions affecting the trip. An unanswered call is awaiting follow-up, not acceptance. This flow does not require a 15-minute in-app offer response.

After staff confirm availability, the selected car and driver are held for those dates for **24 hours** while the client decides. Starting deposit checkout replaces that window with a separate **30-minute** hold from checkout start. Pending staff checks create no hold. Expired holds require a fresh availability check. A hold is not proof of a paid or confirmed booking, and this agreement creates no undisclosed holding fee or cancellation deduction.

Keep confirmed dates accurate and promptly tell staff if availability changes. Fave must resolve the conflict before taking payment or confirming further work. Do not substitute another driver or car yourself. If unavailable, Fave asks the client to choose another car; a replacement after booking requires an explained and agreed arrangement.

Saved client estimates and accepted quotes retain their rates and calculations. Later changes to your profile or car benchmark do not silently reprice them or change your already agreed pay. An extension requires a fresh availability check and an agreed amendment to your work and pay. The client must accept the revised trip total before the booking changes; added lodging nights use the nightly rate saved in the accepted quote and are payable by the client when the extension is accepted. Do not treat an unapproved extra day or detour as automatically agreed.

## 5. Morning payments and records

Fave pays your **full itemized pay for each trip day in the morning**, including that day's applicable lodging amount, from Fave's business funds. This is the complete pay for that day, not an advance towards a routine later balance. There is no separate nightly lodging transfer or trip-long lump sum. The agreed payout method and payment record must identify the day, components, amount and status. A transfer instruction or screenshot alone is not proof that funds arrived.

Report a missing, delayed, failed or incorrect payment promptly. Fave must investigate and correct amounts owed and agree a safe plan with you if a payment problem affects the day's work. You are not required to fund an undisclosed expense or abandon a passenger to resolve a payment dispute. Client collection or a provider delay does not extinguish Fave's payment obligation. These terms do not waive any mandatory due-date or wage protection.

If a trip is cancelled after that day's full payment has been sent, **you keep that day's payment**. Fave stops future daily payments when the trip ends. Fave does not demand a separate return of that day's lodging component for an unused night under this rule. A valid daily payment does not become a mistaken transfer merely because the trip later cancels or ends early. This does not authorize keeping a proven duplicate or genuinely erroneous transfer, such as payment to the wrong recipient or for the wrong amount or day; any correction requires explanation, evidence and a fair opportunity to dispute it, with no automatic debit authority created here.

After the trip, Fave and you reconcile the daily-payment and trip records; there is no routine additional driver balance. This does not erase an unpaid agreed amount, a correction in your favour or a statutory entitlement. Any exceptional adjustment must be separately explained and lawfully agreed or determined. No unspecified commission, chargeback pass-through, penalty, set-off or right to take money from your payment account is authorized by this agreement. Tax withholding may occur where legally required, with an explanation and the appropriate record; neither party may transfer a tax duty the law places on it to the other by assumption.

Fave's client refund duties remain Fave's responsibility even when a driver payment has already been sent. Do not collect client money, arrange an off-platform substitute payment or demand a refund directly from a passenger on Fave's behalf without a separately authorized, lawful arrangement. Fave does not operate a driver wallet or describe these funds as escrow.

## 6. Performing the trip safely

Use only the agreed, eligible driver and car. Hold and maintain the driving entitlement, permissions, insurance and vehicle fitness required for the particular activity. Before work, resolve lawful seating, luggage, child restraints, accessibility needs, itinerary, necessary rest and safe overnight arrangements with Fave. Do not assume an app approval establishes the precise licence class or insurance cover required for a trip.

Drive lawfully and with reasonable care. Do not drive impaired, disqualified or dangerously fatigued, overload the car, or pressure anyone to waive safety requirements. Take necessary safe breaks and tell Fave when timing, vehicle condition or road conditions make the agreed plan unsafe. Treat passengers and staff fairly; do not harass, threaten, discriminate against or exploit anyone. Necessary disability assistance or a safety report is not misconduct.

For an emergency in Uganda, call Police on **999 or 112**, as published by the [Uganda Police Force](https://upf.go.ug/faq/), and follow emergency-service instructions. Contact Fave when safe about a collision, injury, breakdown, harassment, crime or another incident, with the booking reference and necessary facts. Cooperate with lawful reporting and insurance duties. Preserve relevant evidence securely; do not publish another person's identity documents, private contact details or itinerary.

Fave support is not emergency dispatch or a promise of 24-hour monitoring. The app does not provide continuous GPS tracking in this version. A notification is not proof that someone has seen a safety report or dispatched help. Fave and you must take reasonable steps within your respective control to secure a safe outcome; a safety pause, restriction, termination or payment dispute never authorizes abandoning passengers in an unsafe situation. The [client safety policy](client-safety-policy.md) explains the corresponding passenger arrangements.

Use client contact and trip details only for the assigned trip and necessary lawful follow-up. Contact release follows the client's separate choice and verified-booking checks; staff may check availability before private client contacts are released. Do not use these details for marketing, unrelated approaches or public posting. Fave never needs your mobile-money PIN, card PIN, password or authentication code; enter payment authentication only in the payment method's secure prompt.

## 7. Restrictions, leaving Fave and fair review

Fave may temporarily restrict an application, car or account where necessary for a material safety concern, suspected fraud, invalid evidence, legal compliance or a serious breach. The restriction must be proportionate and reviewed fairly. Fave must explain the reason, correction route and conditions for further access unless a lawful security or investigation restriction prevents that disclosure. Urgent protective action may precede notice, but it must not become an unexplained permanent decision.

To challenge a decision or supply corrections, contact [farid@fidexa.org](mailto:farid@fidexa.org), identifying your account/application or booking and the issue. Fave must consider relevant information and explain the outcome. This is a support route, not a promise of an unbuilt in-app appeal feature or a waiver of court/regulator access.

You or Fave may end future participation by notifying the other. Notify Fave promptly if you cannot safely or lawfully complete an existing commitment so it can address the booking. Pending work, safe handover, accrued pay, client refunds, complaints and necessary lawful records must be resolved fairly. Leaving or being restricted does not by itself forfeit earned money, authorize a penalty, erase statutory notice or other rights, or require retention of personal information without a lawful purpose. The privacy notice governs data requests.

Any claim against you must identify proven, reasonably foreseeable loss caused by your breach or fault and the reasonable amount attributable to your conduct, allow you to respond, and account for other parties' responsibility and reasonable mitigation. This does not limit a remedy the law requires. Ordinary vehicle wear is not automatically unlawful damage. This agreement creates no blanket indemnity, automatic liability for client/provider disputes, unlimited legal costs, duty to defend every third-party claim or authority to debit disputed sums.

Neither party excludes liability for death or injury caused by negligence, fraud, deliberate wrongdoing or any other liability or right that cannot lawfully be limited. There is no fixed monetary liability cap here. External services and events do not excuse Fave's or your own fault or remove mandatory remedies. No term transfers Fave's client-service promise or regulatory obligations entirely to you.

Fave must explain material changes and obtain fresh agreement before applying a change that requires it. A later version does not retroactively reduce agreed trip pay or remove accrued rights. Keep a copy of this agreement and your trip/pay records; request a copy or correction from support if needed. This agreement does not require optional marketing consent or prevent a lawful complaint or disclosure.

Ugandan law governs this agreement, subject to mandatory rights. You may use available Ugandan courts, labour or other competent authorities, regulators and lawful complaint routes without first surrendering rights through an internal process or compulsory foreign arbitration. There is no contractual short complaint deadline. An unlawful provision applies only to the extent lawful; the remainder continues only where it can fairly operate. Delay in exercising a right is not an automatic waiver.
